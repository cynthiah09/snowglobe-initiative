Who We Are

A community rooted in care

We're a youth-led environmental initiative working with local businesses, schools, and community members to collect discarded materials and repurpose them into useful, creative products — diverting waste from landfills and reducing our collective environmental footprint.

♻️
Materials Repurposing
We collect plastics, textiles, and other discarded materials and transform them into creative, useful products.
📚
Education & Workshops
Monthly hands-on workshops teach sustainable practices and inspire long-term behavioural change.
🤝
Community Partnerships
We work alongside local businesses, schools, and families to build a network of environmental advocates.

School Clubs

Bring SnowGlobe to your school

Start a student-led SnowGlobe club at your school — a space for ongoing involvement, environmental leadership, and community advocacy. We provide everything you need to get started.

1
Reach Out to Us
Fill out our club interest form and tell us about your school. We'll respond within a week.
2
Get Your Starter Kit
We'll send you a free club starter kit with guides, materials, and activity ideas.
3
Launch & Lead
Host your first meeting! We'll provide ongoing mentorship and support throughout the year.
4
Make an Impact
Run workshops, organize drives, and become a recognized sustainability leader in your school.
